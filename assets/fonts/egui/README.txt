The licences of the fonts egui 0.37 builds into mythicshot.exe (crate epaint_default_fonts 0.37.0,
copied unchanged from its fonts folder). The installer puts each beside LICENSE.txt.

Hack-LICENSE.txt             Hack-Regular.ttf: monospace text. MIT (Hack) and the Bitstream Vera licence.
UFL.txt                      Ubuntu-Light.ttf: letters Manrope lacks. Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0.
emoji-icon-font-LICENSE.txt  egui-icons.ttf, five glyphs of emoji-icon-font. MIT.

egui's Noto Emoji and full emoji-icon-font are built in only with its monochrome_emoji_fonts
feature, which Mythicshot does not turn on.
